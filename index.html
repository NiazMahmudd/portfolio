Flagship · marketing + AI in one systemBuilt

B2B Lead Finding & Personalised Cold Email Outreach

n8nOpenAIAI agentsApifySerperGoogle SheetsResendTelegram

A full outbound sales pipeline in three stages. It finds the right prospects, writes each one a personal email, and sends them on a schedule.

  1. Find leads. An Ideal Customer Profile agent turns a plain request (from Telegram, chat or a schedule) into a search. Apify scrapes matching leads, the workflow waits and re-checks until the run finishes, then upserts the leads to Google Sheets.
  2. Personalise. For each lead it scrapes their LinkedIn profile and company website, then a Personalisation agent writes a tailored email using structured output. Offers and tone are set in one config step.
  3. Send. A scheduled run reads the ready leads, sends each email through the Resend API, updates the sent status, and spaces out sends to protect deliverability.

Why it matters: the research and writing work of an SDR team, done automatically, with every lead tracked in one sheet.
